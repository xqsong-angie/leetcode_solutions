## 💡 How to Approach Company Prep (Crunch Time)

1. **No Pressure, Just Review**: With usually only a week or less before an OA, skip tracking statuses like `❌`, `🙂`, or `✅`. Don't overthink or add unnecessary anxiety!
2. **Focus on Pattern Recognition**: Scan through the problems, focus on understanding the key intuitions, and review core edge cases.
3. **Be Kind to Yourself**: Finishing a single pass of these high-frequency topics is already a massive achievement. Trust your prep!

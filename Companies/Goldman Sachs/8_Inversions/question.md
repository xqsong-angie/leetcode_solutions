**第二题：Inversions**

Given an array arr[] of size n. Three elements arr[i], arr[j] and arr[k] form an inversion of size 3 if arr[i] > arr[j] > arr[k] and i < j < k. Find total number of inversions of size 3.

**Example 1**

* Input: {8, 4, 2, 1}
* Output: 4
* Explanation: The four inversions are (8,4,2), (8,4,1), (4,2,1) and (8,2,1).

**Example 2**

* Input: {9, 6, 4, 5, 8}
* Output: 2
* Explanation: The two inversions are {9, 6, 4} and {9, 6, 5}.
